How it works

  1. Approve USDC and join with the minimum deposit or more. Your join transaction records the hash of the terms below; that is your consent.
  2. Set a route: an amount per 30 days to the Scan Room payee, the only creator this vault routes to. The patron tier is 10 USDC. You can change or remove the route any time.
  3. Once a period, the keeper settles your position: the interest your deposit earned pays the route. Your patron status is computed live: routed at the tier or above, and the yield available right now covers what is owed right now. If it is short, the payee gets less and the status lapses until it is covered. Nothing is ever taken from your deposit.
  4. This pilot is principal-only. Interest beyond your route stays in the position as a buffer and goes to the payee when you leave. You get your deposit back, never the interest. If the source cannot pay the buffer out on the day you leave, your deposit still comes back first and the buffer waits in the contract.
  5. Withdraw part of the deposit or leave whenever you like. No lock, no notice, no exit fee. At the minimum deposit there is no partial withdrawal: it is all or nothing.

What the contract cannot promise: the yield source. If the lending protocol loses money, every depositor loses the same share. There is no owner, no pause and no upgrade in this contract, so there is also nobody who can undo that. Read terms 6 to 10 below before you join.
