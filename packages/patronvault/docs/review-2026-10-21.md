# Cold review, 2026-10-21, and what it changed (v1.1 -> v1.2)

Two reviewers that had not seen the build read PatronVault v1.1 (`main` at 13597e8) cold, each on a private copy, each through one lens: A, money flow and accounting; B, the adversarial surface. Each wrote proof-of-concept Foundry tests for every claim. Their reports and PoCs stayed in the review copies; this file is the record of what they found, what was done about it, and what was deliberately left alone. Findings are numbered as the reviewers numbered them, with the verdict after independent reproduction here.

Verdict going in: ship with fixes. Verdict going out: the fixes below are in, pinned by `test/PatronVault.review.t.sol` and `test/PatronVault.product.t.sol` (101 offline tests, 8 + 9 invariants in two modes), and the pilot month runs against the real source on a fork (`test/fork/PatronVault.aave.t.sol`).

## Found before the reviewers started

The day's first baseline run of the shipped suite on `main` failed `invariant_I6` on a new fuzz seed: `claimYield(claimableYield(m))` reverted `ExceedsYield` with the fee on. Earlier runs (80 green on 2026-10-15 and 2026-10-17) never drew that seed. Reviewer A found the same defect independently (F1 below) with a different counterexample, which is the check a cold review is for.

## Findings and dispositions

| # | Severity | Finding | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | Medium | `claimableYield` reserved n + 1 shares; a settle with a fee rounds up twice per route, so the "lower bound" could be a unit high and `claimYield(claimableYield(m))` reverted. | Fixed: reserve is `(fee ? 2n : n) + 1`, and the same margin comes off the liquidity cap. `test_claimableYield_isAcceptedByClaimYield_withFee_pinned`, `testFuzz_claimableYield_isAcceptedByClaimYield_withFee` (both fail on v1.1, pass on v1.2). |
| F2 | Low | `previewSettle` used the no-fee reserve while `_settle` used the fee reserve: at the boundary the preview said paid and funded, the settle paid nothing and flagged unfunded. | Fixed: same expression. `testFuzz_F2_previewSettleMatchesSettle_withFee`. |
| F3 | Low | Each settle costs the member up to one source share per route to the source's rounding; on a 6-decimal source a block-frequency settler (keeper or griefer) could consume a thin member's whole monthly surplus. | Fixed in the contract: a third party may settle a member once per `settleInterval` (`period / 30`, a day for the pilot); the member any time. The keeper settles once per period. `test_F3_*`. |
| F4 | Low | Healthy-branch `withdraw` at zero yield margin can deliver up to one share plus one unit less than asked while principal is debited in full; the difference stays in the position. | Documented, not changed: debiting by what was delivered would leave the remainder one share under-backed instead. README "Exactness and rounding"; terms clause 3 (v1) / 4 (v1.1). |
| F5 | Low (modeled) | On a MetaMorpho source, Morpho Blue's rounded-up market-share burns can lower the vault's share price by dust on a withdraw, so a buffer-draining settle may leave the position below `previewWithdraw(principal)` by a sub-unit amount until the next interest tick; `inShortfall` reads true, no money moves. Shown on a Morpho-shaped model only. | Not applicable to the pilot (Aave wrapper: price only rises on a round-up withdraw). Listed under fork checks for any Morpho deployment. |
| F6 | Low | A liquidity-short settle forgave up to two units per route through the pro-rata floors; a one-unit route was neither paid nor held. README rule 8 said held, not forgiven. | Fixed: the floors fall on the forgiven side only when yield is short; a liquidity hold holds everything the yield covered. `test_F6_liquidityShortSettle_forgivesNothingTheYieldCovered`. |
| F7 | Info | `isPatron` flickered false when a third party settled seconds after a join (a few units owed, no yield yet); `inShortfall` reads true right after a join at a non-integer price. | The first is closed by F3's spacing (no third-party settle inside the first interval). The second is the deposit rounding, disclosed in README and on the page. |
| F8 | Info | Amounts a route was still owed (held for liquidity or unpaid to a refusing address) were redistributed silently on leave. | Fixed: `_leave` emits `CreatorForgiven(creator, member, owed)` per route. `test_F8_*`. |
| F9 | Info | `Settled.pay` reported the intended payment, not what left (a frozen creator's amount was counted as paid). | Fixed: `Settled.paid` is what actually reached creators. `test_F9_*`. |
| F10 | Info | A route to the vault's own address (or the source) stranded USDC in the vault for good. | Fixed: `InvalidRecipient` for creator, `yieldSink` and `feeRecipient` equal to the vault or the source. `test_F10_*`. |
| F11 | Info | Principal-only members receive sub-share yield dust on partial withdrawals; the terms said "never receive interest". | Terms amended (v1 and v1.1): the rounding is disclosed as rounding, not interest. The pilot's terms hash is therefore new: see below. |
| F12 | Info | A fee recipient Circle freezes strands the fee pool (immutable, `redeem` outside try/catch). Members unaffected. | Documented in README. |
| F13 | Info | Test fidelity: the mock's liquidity cap did not deplete, so the hold path and exits were under-exercised; README's 3.30% sizing was 94.9% on a 365-day year; source views outside try/catch would trap members if a source's views ever reverted; incentive rewards to the vault address are unclaimable. | Mock now depletes its cap on every withdraw and redeem (all invariants still hold). README sizing corrected to 3.31%. Rewards and the view note documented. |
| F14 | High (found here, on the fork) | A caller whose gas limit came from `eth_estimateGas` found the cheapest "success": the source withdraw starved of gas inside the aToken burn, caught by the hold path, `CreatorUnpaid` emitted, transaction successful, nobody paid. The keeper's first fork run did exactly this (253k estimated, 333k real). | Fixed: every caught source call gets exactly `PAYOUT_GAS` (1.5M) and the caller must hold it first or the whole call reverts `InsufficientGas`. `test_F14_*` (mock), `test_fork_settleStarvedOfGas_revertsInsteadOfHolding` (real source). The keeper budgets 2M + 600k per member and never uses the estimate. |

## Reviewer B: adversarial surface

| # | Severity | Finding | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | High | `funded` was reset to true by any settle that found no routes, so a member could remove a route and add it back (two cheap transactions, or one from a smart wallet) and read as a patron until the keeper's next settle while paying nothing. Also true at route creation for a position already in shortfall. | Fixed: a settle with no routes leaves the flag alone, and `isPatron` computes coverage live (yield available now against everything owed now, the settle's own formula); with nothing owed yet it falls back to the last reading, and never for a position in shortfall. `test_H1_*`. The keeper's cadence no longer affects what a gate sees. |
| M1 | Medium | Principal-only mode could not keep yield from a member who routed to a second wallet of their own: the whole exit buffer, fee-free. The README and terms described the member's address, not the member. | Fixed for the pilot and made explicit for the product: an optional immutable `creatorRegistry` (constructor argument; `FixedCreators` is a fixed list with no owner). The pilot deploys with exactly one creator, the Scan Room payee, so routes can only go there. A product vault that onboards creators needs a managed registry behind the same interface; that is ruling R16 in the product doc. Terms clause 2 now says so. `test_M1_*`. |
| M2 | Medium | A one-unit route satisfied `isPatron`, and `creatorMin` never reaches routes that already exist, so a route that lands before the floor is grandfathered. | Fixed in the gate: `isPatronAtLeast(creator, member, minAmountPerPeriod)` carries the gate's own floor; the Worker and the page use it with the pilot's 10 USDC. The terms' promise that a floor binds only new routes is unchanged. Operational rule: creators set `creatorMin` before an address is announced. `test_M2_*`. |
| L1 | Low | The hold path could not tell a refused payment from a source call that ran out of gas; on a source needing more than the stipend, a member's gas-tuned route removal forgave a payable creator. Not reachable on Aave (~200k); conceivable on a fragmented Morpho queue. | Fixed: a caught call that consumed its whole stipend reverts `SourceOutOfGas` for the entire transaction; only a refusal is held. A source that needs more than `PAYOUT_GAS` is loud, never silently unpaid. `test_L1_*`. |
| L2 | Low | A source whose `maxWithdraw` view reverted would trap every routed member and every principal-only member with yield, because every member path reads it. | Fixed: `_liquid()` treats a reverting view as zero liquidity, which holds payments and parks exit buffers; withdraw and leave keep working. `test_L2_*`. |
| L3 | Low | Settle frequency costs the member a share per paying settle; a fresh member settled one block in read unfunded. | Closed by A's F3 spacing and H1's live gate. README rule 7 reworded. |
| L4 | Low | On exit, the creators' buffer was forwarded before the member's principal, so under scarce liquidity a member at the minimum (the whole pilot) had no exit at all until liquidity covered both. | Fixed: `_forwardYield` forwards only what the source can pay beyond the member's own redeem; the rest, and anything a recipient refuses, is parked as `sinkShares` and `forwardSink()` (permissionless) delivers it to the sink later. The deposit comes first, always; nothing parked ever reaches the member. Terms clause 8 says so. `test_L4_*`. |
| I1 | Info | "Held, not forgiven" lasts only while yield covers: a later shortfall settle forgives held carry too. | Documented (README rule 8). |
| I2 | Info | Same as A's F2. | Fixed. |
| I3 | Info | Pilot wiring with the sink equal to the only route: a frozen payee handed every leaver the whole buffer. | Closed by L4's parking: a refused recipient and a refused sink both park, the member never receives the buffer. The runbook uses a separate cold address as the sink anyway. |
| I4 | Info | Constructor accepted the vault, source or token as sink or fee recipient; a frozen fee recipient strands fees. | Fixed (`InvalidRecipient`, token included); the fee-recipient note is in README. |
| I5 | Info | Reentrancy: guard holds; `setCreatorMin` reachable mid-settle (harmless). | No change. |
| I6 | Info | A source that burns more shares than `previewWithdraw` promised would eat up to n principal shares per settle silently. | A fork check (`withdraw(x)` burns exactly `previewWithdraw(x)`) in the runbook; the Aave month on the fork burned exactly the preview. Not enforced in the contract: a one-share revert would stop every payment on a source with dust-level deviations. |
| I7 | Info | Gas nits (`_accrue` writes every carry; a dead branch). | Left. |
| I8 | Info | `_call` rejects garbage returns, tolerates empty. | As intended. |

Operational items from B carried into the runbook: gate on `isPatronAtLeast` with the route amount, floors before announcement, the source has admins (terms clause 4 now says so), keeper gas budgets, pilot members are all-or-nothing at the minimum, deploy-day checks (sink and fee recipient not the vault, source or token; sink not blacklisted; the screen with the pool's liquidity figure), and no sweep for tokens sent to the vault directly.

## Verified sound by reviewer A (not changed)

Principal safety at 16 routes with fee and a skewed price; the reserve counts per path; `claimableYield` with no fee under a liquidity cap and a frozen creator; `withdraw` healthy-stays-healthy and shortfall-ratio-never-worsens; carry arithmetic exact and overflow-safe at uint96 amounts over long gaps; fee only on paid net, fee shares worth at least the fee; leave with 16 routes and frozen recipients returns principal within one join share and never more than dust; `totalPrincipal`, `memberCount`, `creatorPaid` exact; share price monotone after a round-up withdraw on OZ-style and rate-based sources; uint128 headroom.
