{ "intent": { "thesis": "free text, or the structured fields: market, side, probability_basis, size_usd, bounds" } }

free verdict: same engine, same ruleset, not attested, not anchored. Extraction contract stated in every response.
receipt: EAS on Base, attester 0xfB0e…7824, your wallet as recipient. Under schema v3 the verdict is re-derived on chain from the attested tally.
audit, not advice: a reasoning verdict says whether the thesis holds together, never whether the trade wins. The example is a real market and a real thesis; it is not a recommendation. Terms.
