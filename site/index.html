your trade idea

Kalshi, Polymarket or Limitless. Name the venue and the question, or the check will skip it.

What a complete idea includes
  • The market's own terms: the question, the date it settles, and the source that settles it.
  • Your odds and where they come from: the number, a source someone else can check (a dataset, an official source or your own math), and the date you read it.
  • What would prove you wrong: the specific event, before the market settles.
  • Why the market is wrong. “Everyone agrees” is not a reason. This one is advice; it never changes the verdict.

PASS

Nothing failed. The check does not verify your facts, and it does not say the trade wins.

WAIT

The check could not settle part of your idea, and it names which part. Treat it as a no until you fix it.

FAIL

A rule fired on what your idea says. Each reason says what fired and ends with the line to fix.

The rules are fixed code. A model reads your text; it never returns the verdict.
