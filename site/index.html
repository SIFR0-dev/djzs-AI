What a complete thesis includes
  • The market's own terms: the question, the date it settles, and the source that settles it.
  • Your odds and where they come from: the number, a source someone else can check (a dataset, an official source or your own math), and the date you read it.
  • What would prove you wrong: the specific event, before the market settles.
  • Why the market is wrong. “Everyone agrees” is not a reason. This one is advice; it never changes the verdict.
