DJZS. The rules

Every rule the check applies is fixed, published and versioned. The full rules, the hash recipe and the receipt format now live in the docs. This page keeps the three answers, the links, and the place where the outcome study's result will be published.

The three answers three outcomes, never two

PASSNothing failed and the idea is complete. It does not say the facts are true or that the trade wins.
WAITThe check could not settle a fact the verdict depends on. It does not guess. Treat it as a no.
FAILA rule fired on what the idea says. Stop.

Abstain over guess.
