DJZS. A pre-execution gate your treasury can recompute.

OKFORagent-framework builders · DAO treasuries · anyone deploying autonomous capital
OKCHECKthe reasoning behind a move, before the order goes out · PASS / WAIT / FAIL
OKRECORDa permanent public record anyone can check without trusting DJZS
OKPRICE2.00 USDC per check, paid by the agent · no account, no API key

Your agent is about to move money. You already have spend caps. A cap limits the size of a mistake, never whether the position should exist. DJZS checks the reasoning first and leaves a record of it: a verdict computed by fixed code, not improvised by a model, with a receipt a third party can recompute.

Two gates DJZS checks WHY · your wallet stack checks WHAT

Your transaction pipeline already answers is this transaction safe to sign? It cannot answer is the reasoning behind this position sound? A transaction can clear every check and still act on an idea that names no way to be wrong, no source, or only a story.

What you get a record, not a prediction

A verdict nobody can argue with later

A model reads the idea; fixed code decides. The same reading always gives the same verdict.

A receipt anyone can recompute

Every paid check leaves a permanent public record, on Base or Irys, tied to the exact text checked. Check one →

A track record for the agent

Over MCP, name the agent's wallet and each check adds to its public history, which anyone can read for free before they delegate to it.

A no you are not charged for

An idea sent to the wrong check, such as a perp trade sent to the prediction-market check, is refused, and a refusal costs nothing.

What a PASS does not mean read before trusting it

A PASS says the reasoning was complete and free of the listed defects at that moment. It does not say the facts are true, the price is still live, or the trade wins. Those remain yours. What has been measured about the check, and what has not, is in the docs.
