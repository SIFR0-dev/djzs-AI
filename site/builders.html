Two gates: DJZS checks why, your wallet checks what

  1. 1Your agent proposes a trade.A prediction market bet, or a perp trade.
  2. 2DJZS checks the thesis.PASS: it goes on. WAIT or FAIL: it stops and nothing executes.
  3. 3Your wallet checks the transaction.The checks you already run before signing.
  4. 4The order goes out.With a public record that it was checked first.

A transaction can pass every wallet check and still act on a thesis with no way to be wrong, no source, or only a story.

What you get

A verdict nobody can argue with later

A model reads the thesis. Fixed code decides. The same reading always gives the same verdict.

A receipt anyone can check

Every paid check leaves a permanent public record, on Base or Irys, tied to the exact text checked. Check one

A track record for the agent

Name the agent's wallet and each check adds to its public history. Anyone can read it for free before they delegate to it.

A no you are not charged for

A thesis sent to the wrong check, such as a perp trade sent to the prediction market check, is refused. A refusal costs nothing.

What a PASS means, and what it does not

A PASS says the reasoning was complete and free of the listed defects at that moment. It does not say the facts are true, the price is still live, or the trade wins. Those stay with you.

What has been measured about the check, and what has not: in the docs.
